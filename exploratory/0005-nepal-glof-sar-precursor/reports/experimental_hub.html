Nepal — glacier-collapse SAR precursors

Nepal glacier collapse — SAR precursor analysis

Six years of Sentinel-1 radar over the Langtang Lirung face that collapsed on 26 Aug 2026: a morning-pass melt anomaly visible four months out, a 45-face false-alarm test, and an offset-tracking null. One page, plain-language walkthrough with expandable technical panels.

/experimental/langtang-sar-precursors/

Langtang facet watch — live status map

580 computed steep-glacier faces in the Langtang region, each colored by its recent morning-radar anomaly ranked against 2020–25 history. Snapshot dashboard built on the SAR precursor analysis; a rank against the past, not a prediction.

/experimental/langtang-facet-watch/

Central Himalaya — facet watch analyses

2,576 computed steep-glacier faces across the central Himalaya (82–89°E), colored by their 2026-season morning-radar anomaly ranked against 2020–25 history, with the validated detector run on the worst-ranked faces. Built by GEE batch export; a rank against the past, not a prediction.

/experimental/himalaya-facet-watch/

Status. These pages are exploratory. The 26 Aug 2026 Langtang event was an ice/rock avalanche and debris flow; the precursor signal was found looking back at it, and the facet-watch maps rank faces against their own history. A high rank is a reason to look, not a forecast.
