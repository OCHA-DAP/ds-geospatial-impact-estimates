What would you like to do?

Step 1 · once per organisation

Register your organisation

Name, type, default licence and a contact address. Registering puts the contact in the registry and emails a sign-in link, so there is no separate account to create.

Needs: nothing. Start here if you have never submitted.

Step 2 · per product and delivery

Submit an analysis product

Automated or model-derived damage: optical machine learning, SAR change detection, SAR coherence. Defines the product, then the delivery with its two HDX links.

Needs: a registered organisation and a sign-in.

Step 2 · per product and delivery

Submit a reference product

Human-made damage assessment: expert visual interpretation, crowd mapping, field survey. Same form, with the human methods preselected.

Needs: a registered organisation and a sign-in.

How it fits together

Register → open the emailed link → describe the product and paste the two HDX links → submit. The registration is what the sign-in checks against: a link is only sent to addresses that registered. A late AOI, a corrected file or a new version is a new delivery of the same product, not a new registration.
