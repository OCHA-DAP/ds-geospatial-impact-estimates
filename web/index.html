Behind the map

How this map is made

Every source is normalised onto a common Overture building base, with damage aligned to the Copernicus EMS grades and reported coverage-aware — so each admin unit or H3 cell shows the damage fraction within the area a source actually analysed, and the map colours by the highest signal across the sources you've selected.
