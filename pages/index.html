OCHA Centre for Humanitarian Data

Geospatial Impact Estimates

After a sudden-onset disaster, several agencies publish rapid satellite estimates of what has been damaged — and they disagree. This work builds those estimates, and measures how far they can be trusted before they inform a response.

Venezuela Earthquake 2026

The damage-product evaluation after the M7.5 earthquake: the scroll story, the technical brief and the multi-event damage exposure viewer, with the briefings, results deck, write-up and earlier versions one level down in an archive.

Vantor Open Data activation tracker

Which disasters Vantor (formerly Maxar) has released open imagery for, and how many pre- and post-event images each activation has. Rebuilt daily from the public STAC catalog, with a live-drift check on every page load.

/vantor-activations/

CEMS-UNOSAT

The two public flood-label corpora, Copernicus EMS rapid mapping and UNOSAT, harvested and catalogued: the combined label viewer, the CEMS archive ledger, and the superseded CEMS-only viewer.

Experimental analysesPassphrase

Exploratory work outside the damage-evaluation line, kept behind its own passphrase: methods still being tested, not products. Currently the Nepal glacier-collapse radar-precursor pages.

/experimental/

Scope. The paper artefacts are a single-event case study, frozen to the 2026-07-15 data snapshot. They report how these products performed on one earthquake; they are not a general verdict on any of them.

Access. The artefacts are pre-publication. Most open with a shared passphrase and are stored encrypted, so this repository being public does not expose them — but a passphrase is only as private as the people holding it. The experimental pages use a separate passphrase of their own.

Adding a page? Create a directory under pages/ with an index.html and add a card to the hub it belongs to (Venezuela, CEMS-UNOSAT, experimental), or above if it fits none. See pages/README.md.
