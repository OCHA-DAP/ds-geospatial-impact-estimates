Activation detail

For the first post-event image, acquired is the satellite pass and public is when it appeared in the open catalog — the day it became available. Delivery lag runs from the event date Vantor records on the collection to that public date. A dash means the catalog carries no usable event date, or no post-event imagery has been released yet. The public date comes from catalog metadata; the daily tracker also records when it first observes each image (seen.json), independent of that metadata — a ◆ marks activations where the two disagree.

What this counts. Only imagery Vantor has actually published to the open catalog. Activation requests — including declined or announced-but-undelivered ones — have no public record, so delivered collections are a floor on activity, not the whole story.
